Being in the business of 10X things requires specific traits for a leader.

Three of them are key: autonomy, responsibility, and transprarency.

Autonomy is expected at any level, from interns to the most senior level.

Responsibility is about ownership. You own your scope and take responsibility for the good, the bad and the ugly within it.

Transparency brings clarity across the organization. You say what you do, do what you say and default to open.

10X leaders also tend to do the following:

Always keep building. Stay close to the tooling.  Help the team.  Don't stand on the critical path.  

Learn new things all the time. 

Avoid safety margins. Safety margins don't scale: in a chain of command, if everyone takes a 10% margin, you've doubled the estimate.

Over-index on iteration speed and rigor of experimentation. Don't let sloppy work go through.

Run for a marathon, not a sprint. 10X changes don't happen overnight.

Speak your mind in a genuine fashion. Not giving hard feedback is tantamount to disloyalty.

Genuine honesty. People won't remember what you said or what you did, they will remember how you made them feel. Time is the real currency.

Be the change you want to see
